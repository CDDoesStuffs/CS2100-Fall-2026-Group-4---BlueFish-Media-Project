About Us

Founded in 2020, Coffee Cafe started as a little local coffee shop where we hoped neighbors could enjoy some quality time and a great cup of coffee together. We wanted to create an atmosphere that feels just like home, and what we've created does exactly that. Whether you're quickly stopping by during your daily commute, meeting a friend, or working on that financial report for work, Coffee Cafe has a seat pulled up just for you.
